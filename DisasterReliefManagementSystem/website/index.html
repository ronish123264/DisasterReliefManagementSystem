During a disaster the facts scatter faster than the paperwork. DRMS keeps one record of affected areas, shelter capacity, supplies, volunteers, relief requests and missing persons, shared between the municipality, its volunteers and the public.

Who uses it

Admin Full control: shelters, stock, approvals, accounts, summary report.
Municipality Owns disaster status, shelter intake and request approvals for its district.
Volunteer Claims pending requests, keeps availability current.
Citizen Reports disasters and missing persons, requests relief, donates.

Response cycle

The same record carries through all four stages, so nothing restarts at each handoff.

Report A citizen files the event, the missing person or the need. It enters the queue as Pending.
Verify The municipality confirms it against ground reports and sets a status and severity.
Deliver An approved request moves to a volunteer, who claims it and takes stock from a warehouse.
Close Delivered and found are final states. Donations are drawn down against the closed total.

Peril types

Earthquake

Structural damage reports routed to the nearest assessed shelter; road blocks flagged on the disaster record.

Flood

Riverine and urban flood zones with water, food and boat requests tied to the affected wards.

Landslide

Route closures logged so relief movements can be re-planned around blocked corridors.

Forest fire

Fire alerts with volunteer call-out and medical supply requests for the surrounding settlements.
