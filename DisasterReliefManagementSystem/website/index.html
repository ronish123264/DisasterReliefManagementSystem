Nepal faces earthquakes, floods, landslides and forest fires every year. During a disaster it is hard to know which areas are affected, which shelters have space, what supplies are available and which volunteers can help. This system brings all of that into one place.

How It Works

1Report

Citizens report disasters, missing persons and relief needs.

2Respond

Municipalities update shelters, resources and approve requests.

3Track

Volunteers claim requests and everyone sees live status.

4Recover

Donations are tracked until every request is delivered.
